Three ways to fly

Everything in the product maps to Learn, Fly, or Compete.

Learn

Controller basics, hover practice, gates, and a short guided first flight.

Fly

Free Flight, Test Flight, Hangar aircraft, environments, and weather.

Compete

Time trials, ghosts, leaderboards, seasons, and tournaments when you are ready.

Built for stick time

  • Original aircraft with distinct flight feel — not manufacturer clones.
  • Gamepad and FPV controller support, plus keyboard fallback.
  • Browser-based — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari where WebGL allows.
  • Training Academy for structured practice before racing.

Alpha limitations

Not every controller is supported. Flight behavior is an approximation. Competitive integrity relies on server checks — client results can be rejected. This is a simulation, not a substitute for real-world training or local laws.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Guests can onboard, calibrate, train, and Free Fly. Accounts are for sync, leaderboards, and events.

Do you support my DJI controller?

Many DJI-compatible setups work through the Gamepad API in Chromium browsers. We do not claim universal support — use Calibration to verify yours.

Can I fly on a phone?

Browse and set up on mobile. Ranked flight needs a supported control method — you will see a clear warning if not.
